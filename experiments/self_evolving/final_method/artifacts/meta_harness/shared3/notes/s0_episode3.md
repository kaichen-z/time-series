# s0 episode 3: document text checks for correction consistency

Reviewed all shared notes, the current best, the 80 unlabeled Train views, and the 54 permitted visible traces. Earlier work established robust short positive seasonal spikes, a nonnegative floor, and two narrow base-preserving guards. Grid-keyword cap increases repeatedly failed the hidden check; no further cap change was attempted.

The documents for visible `task_179` explicitly say the sensor yields **zero readings** during maintenance, while its extracted multiplier is a positive 2.2. The existing modest-shift rule applies the multiplier even though the document describes the opposite direction. A guard limited to that exact contradiction and the existing modest-shift regime changed only `task_179` across 80 views. Submission `0020_s0` passed the hidden check and raised fitness from 0.260121 to 0.260172, but was not promoted, apparently because the gain was too small.

Visible `task_112` documents explicitly discuss irradiance and sky clearing. Its localized +30% correction was only half accepted by the prior harness. Raising its acceptance to full when an hourly seasonal document contains both `irradiance` and `clear`, for a positive 1.2–1.5 multiplier over 20–35% of the horizon, improved its visible gain from -0.0924 to -0.0666. Combined with the zero-reading guard, submission `0022_s0` reached fitness **0.261068**, passed the hidden check, and was promoted. It changed only `task_112` and `task_179` among 80 views; no runtime errors. Both are single-task effects, so avoid further keyword or threshold tuning without new causal evidence. Two of the episode's five allowed submissions were used.

Consolidated lessons from the other notes: keep the accepted seasonal surge rules but do not generalize them to flat or broad corrections; zero events benefit only with a changed effect cap; long-horizon 4x+ spikes at sub-sigma levels and downward shifts on already tiny bases can hurt. Visible overlap cases are too sparse to justify an aggregation redesign. Existing notes retain submission-level detail; this summary captures the operative constraints. Added `shared/skills/compare_visible_harnesses.py` and updated its README to make future impact audits reproducible from permitted data only.
