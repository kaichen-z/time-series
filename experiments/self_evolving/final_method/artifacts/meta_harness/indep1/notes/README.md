# Shared observations

Read these in order: `i1_episode1.md`, `i1_episode2.md`, `i1_episode3.md`, `i1_episode4.md`. Each records submitted rules, visible effects, and limitations. The accepted harness is `../best_harness.py`; rejected or superseded variants remain in `../harness/` and their results in `../attempts/`.

Across episodes, brief upward events benefited from a larger cap and duration-aware endpoint handling, official holiday notices benefited from guarded reductions, recurring maintenance zeros needed local level filling, and solar forecast collapse benefited from a recent-day fallback. Most gains are concentrated in a few related visible tasks. Avoid treating the exact caps or blend weights as universally calibrated.
